Our story

From Trinidad, to New York, to Fayetteville

Dhan and her husband came from Trinidad, spent about ten years in New York, and made Fayetteville home in 2011. They started with a food truck in 2016, opened their first restaurant, and now cook every plate on Chatham Street with their children and grandchildren beside them. They still drive to New York every few months to bring back the real ingredients.

"I like to cook. I like to eat. I like to feed people, and it's a pleasure when they enjoy the food."

Family run. Military friendly. Made fresh daily.
